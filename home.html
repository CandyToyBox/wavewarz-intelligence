FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

How do I join a live battle? +

Open wavewarz.com and connect a Solana wallet. Phantom is a popular free one. Pick a live battle, hear both songs, then trade the one you believe in before the timer ends. On a phone, open wavewarz.com inside the Phantom or Solflare app browser.

What happens when a battle ends? +

The losing side’s pool is shared out: 50% to traders on the losing side, 40% to traders on the winning side, 5% to the winning artist, 2% to the losing artist and 3% to the platform. Nothing sends itself, so tap Withdraw to collect your share.

How do artists get into a battle? +

Start in Quick Battlez. Connect your Audius profile and queue your songs on the setup page, build your record, then tell us about yourself and we will curate your Main Event, Sundays at 7 PM Eastern.
