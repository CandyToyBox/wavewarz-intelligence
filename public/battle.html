      About WaveWarZ

      WaveWarZ is a live music-battle stage on Solana. Artists go head to head, fans listen and support the songs that move them, and traders can trade the live market while the timer runs. Our mission is to get real support to working musicians — win or lose — and to throw a high-energy show while we do it.

      Watch with us on X, Kick, and Twitch. Replays live on YouTube.
