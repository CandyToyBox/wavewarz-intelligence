Frequently Asked Questions

How is a WaveWarz battle winner decided? +

Main Event battles use a hybrid system combining verified panel judges, live X Space audience polls, and on-chain SOL pool support so neither pure whale volume nor bot voting can rig a result. Quick Battles resolve via fast-action community & chart momentum.

What happens if the artist I backed loses the round? +

WaveWarz is built to keep traders in the game: 40% of the losing pool is returned proportionally to losing side holders, while 50% goes to winning traders and 5–7% rewards the battling artists.

How do I submit my music to battle LUI, Geek, or other roster artists? +

Connect your Solana wallet (Phantom, Solflare, or Backpack) on the WaveWarz DApp or join our weekday 8:00 PM EST X Spaces to claim an open Quick Battle slot.
