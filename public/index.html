FAQ

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

What is WaveWarZ? +

WaveWarZ is a live music battle stage where artists go head to head and the audience helps decide the winner. You can watch the show, back the song that moves you, or trade a live market on each side while the timer runs.

Do I need a wallet to watch? +

No. You can watch the livestream on X, Kick, or Twitch, and you can listen to the tracks, without connecting a wallet. You need a Solana wallet to trade, to launch or host a battle, or to connect an Audius catalog.

How do artists get paid? +

Artists earn 1% of every trade on their chart during a battle. When the battle ends, the losing side’s pool is also split instantly: 5% to the winning artist, 2% to the losing artist, and 3% to WaveWarZ.

What happens when the battle ends? +

Settlement happens instantly. The losing side’s pool is split at once: 40% is added to the winning traders’ pool, 5% goes to the winning artist, 2% goes to the losing artist, 3% goes to WaveWarZ, and the remaining 50% is split among losing traders. Winning traders split their pool based on winning tokens held at the end. Losing traders split their 50% based on losing tokens held.

Do I need to withdraw at the end? +

If you’re holding tokens when the battle ends, yes. Press Withdraw to collect your SOL. Trader winnings are not automatic. If you sold all your tokens during the battle, you may have nothing to withdraw.

How does the price move during a battle? +

Buying increases the token price. Selling decreases it. Traders can try to buy lower and sell higher while the timer runs, but that does not guarantee a profit.

Where can I verify WaveWarZ stats? +

See public stats and activity at wavewarz.info. As of November 10, 2026, that board showed 15.89 SOL paid to artists across 1,701 battles.

How do I apply to battle as an artist? +

For Sunday Featured battles, use the Artist Battle Application Form. For Quick BattleZ, upload public, playable tracks to Audius, connect your catalog on a desktop or laptop, and queue a battle. Details are in the Artist lane of the guide above.

When are the live shows? +

Quick BattleZ livestreams and Sunday Featured nights are listed in Tonight / Next up at the top of this page. We keep that time in ET and use the same schedule on wavewarz.info and @WaveWarZ.

Do I have to be 18 to use WaveWarZ? +

You can watch the show without an account. To connect a wallet, trade, or participate in battles, you must be at least 18, or the legal age in your jurisdiction, per our Terms.

About WaveWarZ

WaveWarZ is a live music-battle stage on Solana. Artists go head to head, fans listen and back the songs that move them, and traders can play the live market while the timer runs. Our mission is to get real support to working musicians — win or lose — and to throw a high-energy show while we do it.

Watch with us on X, Kick, and Twitch. Replays live on YouTube.
