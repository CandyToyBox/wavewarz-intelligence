How a WaveWarZ battle works

Two songs go head to head. You hear both, trade the one you believe in, and every trade pays that song’s artist. When the timer ends the battle settles on Solana and you withdraw.

Six short steps. Each one has a video or a picture of what you will see.

  1. Get a Solana wallet
  2. Hear both and trade
  3. How the winner is decided
  4. Settlement
  5. Withdraw
  6. For artists
Step 01

Get a Solana wallet

WaveWarZ runs on Solana, so you trade from a Solana wallet. Any Solana wallet works. Phantom and Solflare are easy places to start, on your phone or as a browser extension.

Add a little SOL to it. You only need enough for the trades you want to make, plus a tiny network fee.

Never share your Secret Recovery Phrase. WaveWarZ and your wallet’s support will never ask for it.

Get Phantom Get Solflare
Solana mainnetEvery trade and every settlement is recorded ONCHAIN, so anyone can check it on wavewarz.info.
Step 02

Hear both songs and trade

  1. Open a live battle on the homepage or from the Quick BattleZ queue.
  2. Listen to both songs. Each side has its own chart that moves as people trade.
  3. Connect your wallet and trade the song you believe in. Your wallet shows the exact amount before you approve.
  4. Sell any time while the battle is live, if you change your mind.

Battles last different lengths. The timer on the battle page is the one to watch.

Step 03

How the winner is decided

A Quick Battle is decided two out of three. Whichever song takes two of these wins.

Main Event battles run over several rounds. The win counts for the whole event, not for each round.

  • The pollThe live audience votes for the song they liked more.
  • The chartsThe side with more trading at the end takes this one.
  • DJ WavyThe WaveWarZ AI judge listens to both songs and picks one.
2 of 3 wins the battle
Step 04

Settlement: where the losing side’s pool goes

When the timer hits zero the smart contract settles the battle. The winning side keeps its pool. The losing side’s pool is split five ways, the same way in every battle. These percentages are fixed in the contract and can’t be changed.

Losing side’s pool10 SOL
  • Losing side’s tradersBack to the people who held the losing song50%
  • Winning side’s tradersAdded to the winning side, shared by its holders40%
  • Winning artistThe artist whose song won5%
  • Losing artistThe artist whose song lost still gets paid2%
  • WaveWarZKeeps the platform running3%

Each trader’s part is shared by how many shares they held. Amounts are an example, not a promise of what any battle will pay.

1%of every trade goes to that song’s artist, the moment the trade happens.
0.5%of every trade goes to WaveWarZ. That is the whole platform fee per trade.
Step 05

Withdraw after the battle

Nothing is sent to traders on its own. After a battle settles, you collect your part yourself. This works whether your song won or lost.

  1. Open the battle you traded on.
  2. Connect the same wallet that holds your shares.
  3. Press Withdraw and approve it in your wallet.

Step 06

For artists: battle with your Audius catalog

Connect your Audius profile and your songs can go straight into Quick BattleZ. You earn 1% of every trade on your song while the battle is live, then 5% of the losing side’s pool if your song wins, or 2% if it loses.
