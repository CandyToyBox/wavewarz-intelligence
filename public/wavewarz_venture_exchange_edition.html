FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

How do I participate in a live WaveWarz battle? +

Connect any Solana wallet (Phantom, Solflare, or Backpack) at app.wavewarz.com. Choose an active battle, listen to both tracks, and purchase Artist A or Artist B battle tokens using SOL while the timer is active.

What happens if the artist I backed loses? +

WaveWarz protects fans from 100% loss: 40% of the losing pool is refunded proportionally back to losing side holders, while 50% goes to the winning side traders and 5–7% goes directly to the artists.

How do artists apply to battle LUI or Geek? +

Join our weekday X Spaces at 8:00 PM EST or submit your tracks through the WaveWarz DApp portal to enter upcoming Quick Battles and Main Event brackets.
