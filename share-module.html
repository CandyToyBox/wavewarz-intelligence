Fixes the interrupt

Today's popup fires after every trade. Moving the trigger to Withdrawal means it shows up exactly once, when the trader is actually done for that battle.

Fits how people play

Money goes to the artist either way, so a small net loss on a winning side doesn't feel like a loss to most traders. Nine templates mean there's always a true story to post.

Not just P&L

Picked the winner, rode the underdog, hedged both sides, caught a god candle, got judged by DJ Wavy, just claimed your withdrawal — each is its own card, not a forced ROI headline.

Still honest, either way

Every figure — trades, pools, artist payout, factors — pulls from the same onchain-verified history already used on the Battles page. Template changes emphasis, never the numbers.
